林俊儒

國立東華大學法律學系 專任助理教授

任職於國立東華大學法律學系，研究領域為刑事政策、法律與社會、原住民族法。在進入學術機構之前，有6年執業律師經驗，並投入刑事辯護、策略性訴訟。亦曾於NGO 工作，從事立法遊說、公民倡議。

Jun-Ru Lin

Assistant Professor, Department of Law, National Dong Hwa University

Jun-Ru Lin is an Assistant Professor at the Department of Law, National Dong Hwa University. His research fields encompass criminal policy, law and society, and indigenous law. Prior to entering academia, he had six years of experience as a practicing attorney focusing on criminal defense and strategic litigation, and worked with NGOs engaged in legislative lobbying and civic advocacy.
